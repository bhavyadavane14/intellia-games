Realistic Golf Challenge

Combine real physics with quadratic mathematics in a breathtaking 3D golfing adventure! In Realistic Golf Challenge, your shots are governed by algebraic equations ($y = ax^2 + bx + c$, sine oscillations, and exponential arcs). Adjust the vertex, tune the curve coefficients, and watch the ball arc over cliffs and water hazards directly into the cup!

🎮 How to Play & Controls

  • Analyze the Course: Survey the distance to the pin, elevation changes, cliffs, and obstacles.
  • Input Equation Coefficients: Edit the quadratic formula $y = a(x - h)^2 + k$ in the Equation Box to calculate the shot's trajectory.
  • Take Your Swing: Fire the shot to observe the projectile physics in real time. Refine your equation until you sink a hole-in-one!

🎯 Key Mathematical Concepts

  • Parabolic Trajectories: Understand how gravity creates symmetrical quadratic parabolas with distinct vertices and zeros.
  • Vertex Form & Transformations: Learn how altering $a$ changes steepness, and $(h, k)$ moves the curve's peak.
  • Trigonometric & Exponential Functions: Unlock advanced levels requiring sine waves to bypass undulating canyon hills.
