🎮 How to Play & Controls

  • Stay on the Track: Use arrow keys or touch controls to navigate your character along the runner track.
  • Evaluate Equations: As you approach mathematical arches, quickly solve the arithmetic equations shown on each gate.
  • Pass Through the Correct Gate: Steer through the gate showing the correct solution to gain speed boosts and progress to harder tracks!

  • Mental Arithmetic Fluency: Automate quick addition, subtraction, multiplication, and mental number bonds.
  • Reaction Time & Cognitive Reflexes: Train brain agility to rapidly process numbers in motion under timed pressure.
  • Pattern & Error Recognition: Spot correct mathematical formulations instantly without relying on a calculator.
