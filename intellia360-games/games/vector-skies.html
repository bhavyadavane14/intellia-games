Vector Skies: 3D Vector Adventure

Take flight in a 3D coordinate universe! Vector Skies puts you in the cockpit of an exploratory rocket navigating floating islands in the clouds. By adjusting thrust vectors across the X (East/West), Y (Up/Down), and Z (South/North) axes, players master spatial direction, vector magnitude, and coordinate geometry to land safely on the landing pads.

🎮 How to Play & Controls

  • Analyze the Target Island: Locate the floating destination pad relative to your origin (0, 0, 0).
  • Tune Thrust Vectors: Use the on-screen sliders or numeric inputs to set values for $X$ (horizontal), $Y$ (vertical altitude), and $Z$ (depth forward/backward).
  • Launch & Touch Down: Engage thrust to fly along the vector path. Fine-tune your coordinates until you achieve a flawless landing!

🎯 Key Mathematical Concepts

  • 3D Cartesian Space: Visualize and navigate coordinates across three dimensions ($X, Y, Z$).
  • Vector Magnitude & Resultants: Understand how combined directional vectors produce compound 3D trajectories: $|\vec{v}| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$.
  • Directional Angles & Physics: Experience how positive and negative vector components govern direction in space.
